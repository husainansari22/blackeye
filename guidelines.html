Trust & safety

Community Standards

Acctventa is a marketplace for digital products and accounts. These standards protect buyers, sellers, and the platform. Using Acctventa means you agree to follow them. Breaking them can lead to listing removal, wallet holds, account suspension, or reports to payment partners and law enforcement where required.

1. What this marketplace is for

Acctventa is for legitimate, authorized transfers of digital products you have the right to sell — including social accounts, subscriptions, virtual numbers, VPNs, gift cards, and similar digital goods — using escrow so both sides are protected.

  • Sellers must own or be authorized to transfer what they list.
  • Buyers must pay through Acctventa escrow, not off-platform.
  • Everyone must use a real identity they can verify if asked (including Business KYC for merchants).

2. Honesty in listings

Every listing must be accurate. Do not inflate followers, hide recovery risks, reuse screenshots, or describe an account you cannot deliver.

  • Title, category, credentials, and preview link must match the actual product.
  • Preview links must work and show the real account or product.
  • Stock, price, and delivery type (auto vs manual) must be true.
  • If an account has 2FA, email attached, or restrictions, say so before checkout.

3. Prohibited listings and behavior

These are never allowed on Acctventa:

  • Stolen, hacked, phished, or otherwise unauthorized accounts or credentials.
  • Anything obtained by breaking another platform’s rules in a way that harms others (credential stuffing, malware, SIM-swap assistance, social engineering kits).
  • Child sexual abuse material, exploitation of minors, or any sexual content involving anyone 17 or under.
  • Terrorism, violent crime, weapons trafficking, or instructions that clearly enable those crimes.
  • Fraud, money laundering, stolen payment methods, fake bank proofs, or chargeback abuse.
  • Drugs, illegal pharmaceuticals, or unlicensed controlled substances.
  • Personal data dumps, doxxing, or selling other people’s private information.
  • Impersonating Acctventa staff, banks, or other users.
  • Off-platform payment requests meant to skip escrow or fees.

4. How buyers should behave

  • Pay only through Acctventa. Deals completed on WhatsApp, Telegram, or bank transfer outside escrow are not protected.
  • Check the preview, seller rating, and chat answers before you buy.
  • If delivery fails, open a dispute or support ticket with evidence. Do not harass the seller.
  • Do not demand extra credentials that were not part of the listing after funds are released, unless the listing promised them.

5. How sellers should behave

  • Deliver the exact product described, within the stated time.
  • For manual delivery, send login details in order chat so escrow can release fairly.
  • Do not pull back, change passwords, or reclaim an account after it is sold.
  • Complete Business KYC if you want a verified badge. Screenshots of documents are not accepted — use clear camera photos.
  • Do not create multiple accounts to evade a ban, rating, or debt.

6. Chat, reviews, and reputation

  • Keep chats about the order. No spam, scams, or abusive language.
  • Reviews must be about a real order. Fake reviews and review extortion are banned.
  • Do not share malware links or “login pages” that steal credentials.

7. Money, escrow, and fees

Deposits, purchases, and withdrawals go through Acctventa’s wallet and listed payment partners. Platform sales commission and withdrawal fees apply as shown in the app. Attempting to bypass fees, fake a deposit, or open a dispute in bad faith is a standards violation.

8. How we enforce this

We may remove listings, freeze wallets, reverse in-progress orders, require extra verification, or close accounts. Serious cases (theft, fraud, harm to minors) may be reported to the relevant authorities or payment providers. Enforcement is not always public, and we do not have to give attackers a playbook.

If you see a violation, use in-app Support or email support@acctventa.com. Include the listing, order ID, and screenshots.

9. Updates

These standards can change as the product and the law change. Continued use of Acctventa after an update means you accept the new version. Last updated 22 August 2026.
