1. File Manager → public_html → img → trust
2. Create folder "trust" if it does not exist
3. Upload your CAC scan and name it cac-certificate.jpg
4. Refresh https://acctventa.com/company

Recommended: clear JPG/PNG photo or scan, readable text, under ~2MB.
